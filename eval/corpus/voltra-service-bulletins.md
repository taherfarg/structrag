# Voltra Technical Service Bulletins

Bulletins for authorised workshops. Follow the repair exactly as written.

## TSB-2026-007: Door handle does not extend

### Applies To

E5 built January to March 2026

### Symptom

One or more flush door handles stay retracted when the car is unlocked in temperatures below minus 5 degrees.

### Cause

Ice forms around the handle actuator because the drain channel is too narrow.

### Repair

Replace the handle actuator seal with part 5DH-220 and widen the drain channel using tool T-41.

### Labour Time

0.8 hours

## TSB-2026-011: Slow DC charging in cold weather

### Applies To

All E5 with software below 3.2

### Symptom

DC charging power stays below 50 kW in cold weather even after preconditioning.

### Cause

The battery heater target temperature is set too low in early software.

### Repair

Update the vehicle software to version 3.2 or later.

### Labour Time

0.5 hours

## TSB-2026-014: Rattle from rear parcel shelf

### Applies To

E5 hatchback, all build dates

### Symptom

A rattle is heard from the rear parcel shelf on rough roads.

### Cause

The shelf hinge clips loosen over time.

### Repair

Fit two felt pads, part 5PS-018, under the hinge clips and replace the clips with part 5PS-019.

### Labour Time

0.3 hours

## TSB-2026-019: Infotainment screen reboots

### Applies To

E5 with head unit hardware revision B

### Symptom

The centre screen goes black and restarts while driving, typically after 40 minutes.

### Cause

A memory leak in the navigation map service.

### Repair

Update the head unit firmware to version 7.4.1.

### Labour Time

0.4 hours

## TSB-2026-023: Coolant smell in cabin

### Applies To

E5 built before May 2026

### Symptom

A sweet smell is noticed in the cabin after the heat pump runs for a long time.

### Cause

A hose clamp on the cabin heater core can loosen and weep coolant.

### Repair

Replace the clamp with spring clamp part 5HC-301 and top up coolant to the MAX mark.

### Labour Time

1.2 hours

## TSB-2026-027: Tailgate does not close fully

### Applies To

E5 with power tailgate

### Symptom

The power tailgate stops a few centimetres before closing and reopens.

### Cause

The anti-pinch sensor strip is too sensitive after a production calibration error.

### Repair

Recalibrate the anti-pinch sensor with diagnostic routine TG-CAL-2.

### Labour Time

0.5 hours

## TSB-2026-031: Wind noise from driver window

### Applies To

E5 built February to June 2026

### Symptom

Wind noise from the top of the driver's window above 90 km/h.

### Cause

The upper window seal was fitted out of position at the factory.

### Repair

Refit the upper seal using alignment gauge T-55; replace the seal (part 5WS-112) if it is deformed.

### Labour Time

0.7 hours

## TSB-2026-036: Phone key not recognised

### Applies To

All E5 with app version below 5.0

### Symptom

The car does not unlock when approached with a phone key, although the app shows it is connected.

### Cause

A Bluetooth pairing token is not refreshed after phone operating system updates.

### Repair

Ask the customer to update the Voltra app to 5.0 or later, then delete and re-pair the phone key.

### Labour Time

0.2 hours
